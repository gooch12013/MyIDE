# Prompt guide: Seedream (ByteDance)

Guidance drawn from published Seedream 4 prompting guides (promptus.ai, veed.io, segmind). Covers seedream_v4_5 and the 5.0 models. Edit freely.

- Write clear, natural sentences: subject, action, environment. Concise and precise beats stacked ornate adjectives.
- Five parts in order: subject, style, composition, lighting, details.
- Add colour, lighting and composition only where they matter.
- Editing a reference: action + subject + details ("Replace the background with a pale sand texture, keep the shell and its shadow unchanged").
